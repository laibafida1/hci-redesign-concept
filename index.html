Edial Kennels & CatteryESTABLISHED 1979 · BURNTWOOD, STAFFS

A second home for your dog or cat, kept by the same family since 1979.

Edial is a small boarding kennels and cattery on Lichfield Road, Burntwood. It's licensed by Lichfield District Council, and run by people who have looked after other people's pets for over forty-five years.

Dog kennels

Forty refurbished kennels, each with a covered bedding area and an outdoor run onto grass or open fields.

Bedding & run
Every kennel has its own sheltered bedding area plus an outdoor run with a view over grass or fields.
Exercise routine
Twice a day in summer, three times in winter, around 7am, 11am and 4–5pm.
Toy dog courtyard
Smaller breeds get their own separate courtyard and grass area, away from larger dogs.
Winter heating
Provided through the winter months at no extra cost.

Note: bring your dog's own treats and bedding if you'd like. Inspections are welcome 9–11am, Monday to Saturday.

Cattery

Eighteen penthouse-style cat pens, each a roomy 16ft x 3ft, light and airy, with bedding and extra shelving.

Penthouse pens
Each pen includes a bedding area, ladders and extra shelving for climbing and resting.
Family pens
Three large family pens for cats from the same household who'd like to share.
Heated bedding
Every shelf has a heated pad through winter, included in the daily rate.
Food provided
Whiskas or Felix wet pouches, and Go-Cat or Whiskas dry, or bring your own if your cat has a specific diet.

Note: a current vaccination certificate for Cat Flu (FHV) and Feline Enteritis (FPV/FVRCP) is required at drop-off.

Naylenor Yorkshire Terriers

Alongside the kennels, the Naylor family has bred award-winning Yorkshire Terriers under the Naylenor prefix since the early 1970s.

1971

Ch. Brave Warrior of Naylenor, the family's first champion.

•

Ch. Blue Monarch, Ch. Regal Warrior and Ch. Battle Cry, who went on to also become a Japanese Champion.

1979

Ch. Magic Moment became Top Female Yorkie in Britain.

•

Magic Moment's son, Ch. Naylenor Crown Jewel, and his son, Ch. Naylenor Jewel in the Crown, continued the line.
